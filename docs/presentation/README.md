## What the video covers, in order

1. **The problem, in the first 45 seconds.** A prepaid customer goes quiet. No
   complaint, no cancellation — just a reload that never comes.
2. **The working prototype.** The ranked queue, then one customer opened: the
   score, the evidence behind it, the named tower.
3. **One complete customer journey.** Detect → diagnose → the offer in the
   customer's own language → what happens next.
4. **The two failures a panel found, and the fix.** The holiday customer we
   would have chased, and the sales representative we would have held back
   forever.
5. **The number.** Blanket discounting is worse than doing nothing.
